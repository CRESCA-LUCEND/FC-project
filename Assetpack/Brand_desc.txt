"Fizzy & Crush" is a creamy soda brand in 500 ml cans. It pairs bright fruit with smooth vanilla, in two flavours: Lemon & Vanilla and Rhubarb & Vanilla. The look is playful and retro: a bubbly rounded wordmark over a flowing cream "melt" pattern, with each flavour owning a bold colour (lemon pink, rhubarb blue). Six-packs come in a printed kraft carton with die-cut logo windows, so the cans show through.
